The Women Empowerment Cell (WEC) of Anna University Regional Campus-Tirunelveli was inaugurated on 03.11.2015. The objective of this cell is to create knowledge about the rights of Women, status in the society and to advocate different ways for self improvement and to ensure safety and security for women in the campus.

The Women Empowerment Cell conducts guest lectures and programmes connected to Women Empowerment by inviting prominent Women personalities. Every year Women Empowerment Cell conducts various competitions such as cooking without fire, singing, Rangoli, Mehandhi, Quilling art Jewellery, vegetable carving, Art from Waste and Elocution for the girl students and prizes are distributed to the winners on every International Women's day.

The WEC organizes various seminars and awareness programmes in the campus and the womens day is celebrated on March 8 every year. WEC conducts many competitions for the womens day celebrations.
