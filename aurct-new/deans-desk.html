Welcome Message

Warm Greetings!

With great honor and pleasure I extend my best wishes to All in All respect. In an ancient population, more than 2,000 years old globally, we offer excellent technical education in the field of Engineering Technology and Management.

Our mission is to promote the core values of Science, Engineering, Technology and Management and foster a quality focused teaching learning environment that is conducive for producing tomorrow's leaders in Science, Engineering, Technology and Management.

We encourage our students to innovate on research to advance technology in their discipline, and transform knowledge into products and services to benefit the economic and social development in the region considering the sustainable development without compromise on the quality.

We are embarking on a journey of building a future world. Our Faculty surpassed numerous dimensions through teaching-learning, research, patent publication and outreaching the society with sustainable solutions. On the other hand, our students are excelled in a number of national and international competitions and have brought numerous laurels to the Institution.

I am deeply grateful for the presence of an outstanding group of teaching & non-teaching staff members and knowledgeable students who all have worked tirelessly to ensure the best possible & effective learning experience and quality administration and excellent research culture at our Campus.

I wish everyone for achieving their best in all their future endeavors.

All the Very Best to All!
