About Alumni Association

The Alumni Association of Anna University Regional Campus - Tirunelveli serves as a bridge between the institution and its graduates. Since the establishment of the campus in 2008, we have produced thousands of successful engineers and professionals who are serving across the globe.

Objectives

  • Foster a strong bond between the institution and its alumni
  • Facilitate networking among alumni members
  • Support current students through mentorship programs
  • Organize alumni meets and reunions
  • Provide career guidance and placement support
  • Contribute to the development of the institution
