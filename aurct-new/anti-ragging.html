What Constitutes Ragging?

  • Any act of physical or mental abuse
  • Teasing or handling with rudeness
  • Forcing to do any act which causes shame or embarrassment
  • Asking students to perform tasks against their will
  • Financial extortion or forceful expenditure
  • Any act that affects the mental health and self-confidence
  • Verbal abuse, intimidation, or threats

Punishment for Ragging

  • Suspension from attending classes
  • Withholding/withdrawing scholarship/fellowship
  • Debarring from appearing in examinations
  • Withholding results
  • Suspension/expulsion from hostel
  • Cancellation of admission
  • Rustication for a specific period
  • Expulsion from the institution and debarring from admission
  • FIR with local police
