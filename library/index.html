About Our Library

Our Central Library is located in the Main Block and is spread over 404 sq.m. The Library has a good collection of books covering various branches of Engineering, Humanities & Science and other books related to Competitive Examinations.

The Library has Acquisition Section, Technical Section, Circulation Section, Reference Section, Periodical Section and Digital Library Section. The Reading Hall can accommodate around 120 members at a time.

Features

Modern Library Infrastructure

Equipped with automation, digital resources, and institutional memberships to support academic excellence.

Library Automation

Fully automated with Modernlib software and barcode technologies for circulation and stack maintenance. Includes Enquiry, Circulation, and Administration modules with OPAC (Online Public Access Catalogue).

Digital Library

11 multimedia systems with internet connectivity, providing access to digital collections including e-books, e-journals, and online academic resources for students and faculty.

Institutional Membership

Member of DELNET (Developing Library Network) providing access to inter-library loan services and shared resources across a vast network of participating libraries.
